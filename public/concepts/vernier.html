(The atelier)

We build instruments people pass down, not products people replace.

Case, dial and movement are designed together, so tolerances are set for the whole watch rather than for each part on its own. The result is easier to service and built to keep time for decades.

In-house atelier

Every step under one roof.

We don't outsource the parts that decide how well a watch keeps time. Machining, finishing, regulation and testing all happen in Biel.

01

Machining hall

Five-axis milling and Swiss-type lathes for cases, plates and bridges.

Cases · plates · bridges
02

Finishing bench

Hand bevelling, Geneva stripes and perlage on every visible surface.

Angles · stripes · perlage
03

Regulation lab

Each movement is adjusted in six positions and three temperatures.

6 positions · 3 temps
04

Test chamber

Pressure, condensation and power-reserve checks on every finished watch.
